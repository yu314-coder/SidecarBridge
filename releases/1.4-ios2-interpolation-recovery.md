# SidecarBridge iOS/iPadOS 1.4 (2)

Prepared September 22, 2026 as the follow-up to iOS/iPadOS 1.4 (1).
The macOS companion remains 1.4 (106).

## Frame interpolation recovery

- Replaced the serial per-frame VideoToolbox wait with bounded asynchronous
  hardware decoding, so a normal 60 FPS burst does not exhaust a four-frame
  submission queue.
- Temporary decoder pressure now preserves the last good image, requests a
  fresh H.264 keyframe from the Mac and recovers automatically. The user no
  longer has to turn the experimental switch off and on.
- The recovery keyframe is also passed to ordinary playback, keeping a valid
  fallback decoder root without flushing the viewer or interrupting input.
- Twelve in-flight decoder submissions absorb short bursts while retaining a
  strict memory bound.
