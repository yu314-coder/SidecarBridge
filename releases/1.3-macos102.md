## 4K/60 frame-time stability

- Native 4K/60 now prioritizes predictable VideoToolbox completion speed.
- Added a bounded one-frame encoder cushion, approximately 16.7 ms at 60 FPS, to absorb isolated hardware scheduling stalls.
- Normal-memory 4K/60 allows eight bounded asynchronous hardware submissions instead of six.
- Warning pressure contracts the pipeline to four surfaces and critical pressure to three.
- The previous jitter-tolerant ScreenCaptureKit gate remains in place.

These changes target a 1% low above 50 FPS while retaining native 3840 × 2160 output. Physical results still depend on the Mac hardware, the local link, and iPad decoding capacity.
