# SidecarBridge macOS 1.3 (103)

## Optimization

- Replaced the direct-LAN sender's per-frame `Array.removeFirst()` with a head-indexed ordered H.264 queue.
- This makes normal dequeue constant-time and avoids repeatedly shifting and retaining large encoded 4K packets at 60 FPS.
- Queue compaction is infrequent and bounded: it occurs only after at least 32 consumed entries and after at least half of the backing array is stale.
- Existing frame-count, byte, dependency-chain, keyframe recovery, and back-pressure limits remain active.
