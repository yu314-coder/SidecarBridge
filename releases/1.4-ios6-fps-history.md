## Developer FPS history

- Added a 30-second FPS history chart to the hidden Developer settings.
- Tap the Version row in Settings seven times to unlock Developer options.
- Cyan is frames received on iPad; green is submitted frames, including generated frames; the orange dashed reference is the iPad display refresh rate.
- The chart is diagnostic telemetry, not a promise that the stream reaches or exceeds the display's refresh rate.
